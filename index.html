Privacy Policy — Zexa: AI Study Companion

Effective Date: November 26, 2026 • Package Name: com.zexa.studyai • Developer: Sibnath Bairagi (Sibora)

This Privacy Policy explains how Zexa: AI Study Companion ("Zexa", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, processes, and protects information when you use our Android mobile application. We built Zexa with a privacy-first, no-login architecture so students can practice and generate AI quizzes without creating an account or sharing personal contact details.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

A. Anonymous Device Identifier & Anti-Abuse Security Data

  • What is collected: Upon installation, the app generates a random, anonymous device identifier (X-Device-Id). When requests reach our API server, a one-way salted cryptographic hash (SHA-256) of your network IP address is temporarily computed.
  • Why it is used: Strictly for core app functionality and fraud prevention—specifically to allocate your one-time free trial AI credits, track remaining credits, enforce per-minute rate limits, and link active Google Play subscriptions to your device without requiring an email or password.

B. Camera Permission & Study Note Images (Photo-to-Quiz)

  • Permission Used: android.permission.CAMERA and Android Photo Picker (PickVisualMedia).
  • How it works: Camera and gallery access are strictly optional and triggered only when you tap Scan Notes or upload a study diagram. The selected image is compressed locally on your device and transmitted over encrypted HTTPS to our backend to extract text/concepts and generate practice questions.
  • Retention: Images are processed transiently in memory solely to generate your requested quiz and are never permanently stored on our servers or used to train public models.

C. User Prompts, Exam Selections & Local Study Progress

  • On-Device Storage: Your chosen exams, subjects, chapters, quiz attempts, scores, bookmarks, daily study streaks, language settings, and theme preferences are stored locally on your Android device using Room Database and Jetpack DataStore.
  • Quiz Generation Inputs: When you request an AI-generated quiz, your selected exam title, subject, chapter, difficulty, language, and optional note image are sent to our AI processor to return 10 practice questions.

D. In-App Purchases & Subscription Verification

  • What is processed: If you subscribe to a Monthly or Annual plan via Google Play Billing, Google Play provides an anonymous purchase_token and subscription status.
  • What we do NOT collect: All payments are handled exclusively by Google Play. Zexa never receives, processes, or stores your credit/debit card numbers, UPI IDs, bank account details, or billing address.

2. Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell, rent, or trade user data to advertisers or data brokers. Zexa uses only the following essential infrastructure providers to operate the app:

  • Google Gemini API (Google LLC): Processes text prompts and user-submitted note images over encrypted HTTPS solely to generate educational practice questions.
  • Cloudflare Workers & D1 (Cloudflare, Inc.): Provides edge API routing, rate limiting, and anonymous credit/subscription balance storage.
  • Google Play Billing & Google Play Developer API (Google LLC): Processes subscription payments and verifies active purchase entitlements.

3. Data Security

All communication between the Zexa Android app, Cloudflare Workers, Google Gemini APIs, and Google Play services is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS 1.3. API requests are authenticated using application keys, and IP addresses used for rate-limiting are cryptographically hashed with a server-side salt.

4. Data Retention & How to Delete Your Data

1. Delete Local App Data Instantly: You can erase all locally stored quiz history, bookmarks, streaks, and preferences at any time by clearing the app's storage in your Android device settings (Settings > Apps > Zexa AI > Storage > Clear Data) or by uninstalling the app.

2. Request Server-Side Record Deletion: If you wish to have your anonymous device credit record or subscription link deleted from our backend database, email us at sibnathbairagi94@gmail.com with the subject line "Zexa Data Deletion Request". Verified deletion requests are completed within 7 business days.

5. Children's Privacy & Age Compliance

Zexa: AI Study Companion is an educational revision tool intended for students aged 13 years and older preparing for school, university, and competitive examinations. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information (such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or precise location) from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.
